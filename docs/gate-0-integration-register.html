01 · Authority

Range-local names are retired at integration

This register resolves the cross-range naming conflict found by independent review. Names such as CORE.*, CMP-*, PLANT-*, SRC-*, INFRA.*, AGND, and VCC_15 remain quotations from draft annexes only. They do not become aliases or additional electrical nets.

Canonical syntax

Dots express hierarchy. Uppercase names identify physical objects and named nets. A socket, its occupant, and its passive network are distinct identities. Configuration roles never rename physical objects.

06 · Uniform state classes

Objects cannot disappear between documents

  • Persistent-installed: physically on the chassis and part of the rail/load manifest.
  • Persistent-inactive: still installed and safely powered/terminated; grey is only a rendering attribute.
  • Configuration-only fixture: appears in both matched schematic and netlist for that measurement, but is not inherited as chassis hardware.
  • Removed-off-circuit: retained in physical inventory or parts tray but absent from the electrical graph.
  • Reserved-unpopulated: permanent socket/interface exists; no device instance or hidden signal connection exists.
  • Deferred: excluded under D-01 through D-04 and never used to excuse missing present electrical connectivity or safety requirements.

07 · Dependency order

Gate 0 closes in this order

  1. Approve W02 sign/topology and W03 reference/adaptation structure.
  2. Approve the dedicated W06 oscillator integrator and the stable socket/occupant model.
  3. Bind the W05 regulator topology and operating envelope sufficiently to own its later twin/compensation dependencies.
  4. Approve W07–W10 as incremental states of one exact Figure 9.1 transition map.
  5. Approve W12 passive reconfiguration, multiplier direction, and retained-module ownership of the regulator twin.
  6. Approve W13 exact DUT/loop selection; defer numeric compensation until the loop exists.
  7. Complete the sourced rail-load budget before freezing W00 protection values.
