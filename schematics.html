Week 1 — computing core lands

  • Figure 1.2a inverter (INV)
  • Figure 1.4 summer (SUM)
  • §1.2.3 integrator (INT1)
  • Empty reserved sockets INT2, INT3

everything this week is new

Build all three. Measure each Vo/Vi. Do not patch an ODE yet.
