Week 7 · Input pair and tail source

The final 2N5963 Q1/Q2 matched pair, 2N3707 Q3 tail source, collector/balance network, and common-bias parts are installed. Equal 0 V base fixtures and 10 MΩ collector measurements support balance, offset, and drift observations.

INTENTIONALLY NONFUNCTIONAL
Do not run the computer loop. AMP1 has no amplifier output, the original LM301A is off-circuit, and the inherited INT1 feedback capacitor is isolated. “Hold-capacitor wander” may be observed, but it cannot be attributed to amplifier input bias without a complete current path.

Week 8 · Transitional Q1–Q11 amplifier

Q4–Q11, the high-gain/current-source load, TIS58 buffer, two-diode output bias, complementary output devices, rail bypassing, and 22 Ω output resistors are added. A socketed 47 pF capacitor is a conservative proposed bring-up value—not a copied historical Week 8 value.

FUNCTIONAL · NO CURRENT LIMIT
Safety boundary. Q12/Q13 current limiting is not installed until Week 9. Use externally current-limited ±15 V rails, verify every quiescent operating point before feedback, and do not short the output.

W08.OPEN_LOOP

Microvolt differential fixture and 10 MΩ load for operating-point/crossover inspection. Practical gain measurement still requires a centering/null procedure; ordinary open-loop drive will saturate.
