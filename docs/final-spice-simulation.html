Model tier

How the op-amps and plant were represented.

Behavioral op-amp

OPAMP_LP contains a voltage-controlled differential-gain source, one RC dominant pole, and a smooth tanh output limiter near ±14.5 V. It does not reproduce an LM301A or the Q1–Q13 discrete amplifier.

EERR NRAW 0 INP INM {A0}
RDOM NRAW NPOLE 1k
CDOM NPOLE 0 {1/(2*pi*FP*1k)}
BOUT OUT 0 V={14.5*tanh(V(NPOLE)/14.5)}

REG1 error amplifier: A0=30, FP=10 kHz. SUM1 and INT2: A0=100,000, FP=10 kHz.

Other ideal-tier models

  • Generic NPN pass transistor: finite β, Early voltage, junction capacitances, and transit time.
  • Generic 10 V zener reference and generic diode cards.
  • Voltage-controlled load switch with 0.1 Ω on-resistance and 1 TΩ off-resistance.
  • OSC1 represented by behavioral square and triangle sources, not its complete Schmitt/integrator circuit.
  • ±15 V computing rails and an 18 V unregulated regulator input are explicit.

The current-to-voltage scale is stated unambiguously as S_VI = 100 V/A: one simulated volt represents 10 mA. This resolves the earlier dimensional ambiguity in the label I_SCALE.

Campaign method

Stimulus, numerical method, calibration, and grading.

Stimulus

OSC1 square: 0–5 V, 50 ms high, 100 ms period, beginning at 50 ms. OSC1 triangle: approximately ±5 V at 10 Hz.

Transient solution

tran 50u 350m with Gear integration, reltol=1e-5, abstol=1e-10, and vntol=1e-7.

Calibration

REG1 droop and 63.2% time are measured first. A 1 µF INT2 capacitor then gives derived leaky-twin resistors of 501.209 Ω and 5.326 kΩ.

The configured values—496.549 Ω and 5.276258 kΩ—came from the earlier calibration sample embedded in the deck. The rerun-derived values differ by less than 1%, inside the declared 2% gate.

Interpretation discipline

What was demonstrated—and what was not.

Demonstrated

  • The enhanced final-system deck parses, converges, and completes under ngspice 47.
  • OSC1 supplies correctly ranged square and triangle functions.
  • The ideal-tier regulator produces the expected polarity and a finite, measurable load droop.
  • The simulation-derived twin values reproduce REG1’s first-order droop response.
  • OSC1, REG1, SUM1, and INT2 function together in one transient campaign.
  • The result is reproducible from a pinned executable, deck, runner, and hash-bearing receipt.

Not demonstrated

  • Transistor-level operation of the Q1–Q13 AMP1 or an LM301A macromodel.
  • Historical-device accuracy, exact package pin behavior, matching, leakage, or temperature dependence.
  • Input offset/bias, CMRR, PSRR, noise, common-mode range, slew rate, output-current limiting, or saturation recovery.
  • Real compensation stability margins, loop gain, phase margin, or capacitive-load boundary.
  • Monte Carlo tolerance, startup extremes, supply variation, fault behavior, or thermal performance.
  • Agreement with a constructed chassis. Hardware measurements remain the required final calibration and validation step.

Defensible claim: the final machine’s intended mathematical signal flow and regulator-twin function work in the declared ideal-tier model. This is not yet a prediction of physical hardware performance.
