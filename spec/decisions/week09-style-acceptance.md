# Week 9 schematic style acceptance

Status: APPROVED by the user on 2026-08-15.

The Week 9 visual language is the publication baseline for subsequent capstone schematics:

- conventional black electrical symbols and wires on white;
- coral reserved for the active experiment/change emphasis;
- grey for installed but inactive hardware;
- one large functional sheet where readable;
- a separate device/detail sheet when pin, bypass, compensation, or transistor detail would obscure the experiment topology;
- explicit junctions and insulated crossings;
- semantic SVG and SPICE projected from the same canonical circuit graph;
- physical patch-cord routing remains deferred and is not silently represented as electrical authority.

This approval accepts the presentation language. It does not waive source, connectivity, simulation, or build-readiness gates for later circuits.
