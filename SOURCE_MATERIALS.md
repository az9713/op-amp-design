# Source materials not redistributed

This project was developed against local source representations that are intentionally excluded from Git:

## Roberge textbook

- Local filename used during development: `op_amps_roberge.pdf`
- Role: principal historical source for figures, equations, section references, and circuit behavior
- Excluded because: the repository does not establish redistribution rights for the complete textbook

Place a lawfully obtained copy at the repository root under that filename if you want the historical HTML records' original local links to resolve.

## Extracted book figures

- Local directory used during development: `figures/book/`
- Role: visual comparison and transcription evidence
- Excluded because: these are cropped representations of the textbook

The canonical graphs, decision records, and generated project schematics remain available without these images. One historical crop named `fig-11.18.png` was discovered to depict Figure 11.19 and is explicitly rejected as Week 11 electrical authority.

## Saved Arrow article

- Local filename used during development: `Top 10 fundamental operational amplifier circuits.html`
- Associated browser assets: `Top 10 fundamental operational amplifier circuits_files/`
- Role: peripheral background reference only
- Excluded because: saved third-party webpage content and its cache are not required to reproduce the canonical circuits

This article never overrides Roberge, a primary device source, an approved project decision, or the canonical circuit graph.

## Device models

The model registry records provenance and redistribution limits. Do not add vendor or third-party SPICE models unless their license permits repository redistribution and their exact version/hash is recorded.
